# Proof-First Revenue Loop

## Mission

Hermes uses this skill to convert observable AI leverage into paid, verified outcomes while minimizing speculative building.

The permanent reflex is:

**Create useful evidence before asking for trust. Measure the real result. Scale only verified winners.**

This skill is an orchestration layer. Reuse existing research, SEO, design, video, site, coding, outreach, analytics, and memory capabilities rather than duplicating them here.

When PAULIS-PLACE canonical context is available, `icm/instructions/PROOF_FIRST_REVENUE_LOOP.md` is the policy source of truth. This skill is the portable Hermes execution contract.

## Triggers

Use when:
- the active goal is revenue, customers, leads, sales, acquisition, growth, or retention;
- Hermes is asked to find business opportunities;
- a prospect/client has an observable public AI, creative, web, search, automation, or conversion gap;
- outreach needs a proof asset rather than a generic pitch;
- a proof/test has returned results that need verification and a commercial next step;
- `do smart things` selects revenue work as the highest-value action.

Do not use this to create a fourth active workstream or to justify building a new product without a customer/proof path.

## Hard invariants

1. Proof before pitch.
2. Cash before more code.
3. Inspect before creating.
4. Reuse before adding tools, agents, repos, or integrations.
5. Public competitor material is for pattern analysis, not copying.
6. Never fabricate performance, testimonials, customers, provenance, or guarantees.
7. Define a KPI/baseline/threshold before declaring a test successful.
8. Builder cannot approve itself; material client-facing proof receives independent review.
9. External sends, publishing, ad spend, contracts, production changes, and meaningful spend require the applicable approval/standing policy.
10. Scale volume only after evidence.
11. Preserve owner/client control of code, domains, hosting, accounts, credentials, data, and consequential decisions.
12. Keep tactical prices, providers, lead counts, channels, and cadences configurable. They are experiments, not identity.

## State machine

`SCAN -> QUALIFY -> MODEL -> PROVE -> JUDGE -> APPROVE -> TEST -> VERIFY -> CLOSE -> SCALE -> COMPOUND`

A state transition requires a receipt. Missing evidence means remain in state, iterate, or PARK.

### 1. SCAN

Find evidence-backed opportunities using public or authorized sources. Prefer prospects showing both a visible problem and ability to act.

Useful signals:
- active ad/content/customer-acquisition effort;
- growing/funded/business-budget signals;
- weak creative or conversion execution relative to competitors;
- manual work with obvious measurable automation value;
- a broken lead-response/search/web path;
- a warm relationship with a known business problem.

Output: candidates with evidence refs and the hypothesized business constraint.

### 2. QUALIFY

Score on:
- severity/value of problem;
- ability/willingness to pay;
- time to measurable proof;
- reachability of decision-maker;
- fit with capabilities already owned;
- clarity of KPI;
- brand/legal/privacy risk.

Default bounded slice: 10 qualified targets -> top 3 for proof production. Increase volume only when quality and response evidence justify it.

### 3. MODEL

Inspect the prospect and market. Extract originalizable patterns:
- hook;
- offer;
- CTA;
- proof structure;
- content/ad format and length;
- recurring themes;
- long-running public ad/content signals where observable.

Never clone protected creative, branding, likeness, or copy. Produce an original brief and record provenance.

### 4. PROVE

Produce the smallest useful asset tied to the hypothesized constraint. Examples:
- AI video/UGC sample;
- original ad concept/storyboard;
- landing-page rescue concept;
- SEO/search proof brief;
- lead-response automation demo;
- website or workflow prototype;
- bounded before/after audit evidence.

Do not build a whole product to win permission to test an idea.

### 5. JUDGE

Route to reviewers different from the builder.

Score:
- customer value;
- taste/non-generic quality;
- factual/evidence integrity;
- brand fit;
- security/privacy;
- sovereignty;
- commercial clarity.

Recommended quality floor: 8.5/10. Reject/correct rather than rationalize weak work.

### 6. APPROVE

Before an external action, prepare an approval packet:
- target;
- proof asset;
- exact claims/message;
- channel;
- intended recipient/audience;
- cost/spend ceiling;
- KPI/test plan;
- risk tier;
- rollback/stop condition.

Drafting is not sending.

### 7. TEST

Use a real owned/authorized environment when possible. Define before launch:
- baseline;
- variant/creative ID;
- attribution method;
- window/budget;
- primary KPI;
- pass/fail threshold;
- stop condition.

### 8. VERIFY

Read actual outcomes. Appropriate KPIs may include:
- substantive replies;
- booked calls;
- CTR;
- CVR;
- CPA/CAC;
- ROAS;
- lead response time;
- qualified leads;
- hours saved;
- revenue/retention where directly attributable and authorized.

No real measurement -> no winner claim.

### 9. CLOSE

Use evidence to recommend the smallest paid next step:

- **Vibe Audit** — prove/diagnose constraint.
- **Vibe Rescue Sprint** — bounded fix/test.
- **Sovereign Launch** — owned production system after proof.
- **MAXX Operations** — ongoing measured optimization after production proof.

Never force a sale when the evidence says another problem is more important.

### 10. SCALE

Only after a verified pass:
- generate additional creative variants;
- expand qualified outreach;
- add creator/worker capacity;
- automate repeatable steps;
- increase channel/spend within approval policy;
- raise price based on demonstrated value.

Every scale step has a ceiling and rollback.

### 11. COMPOUND

Propose reusable learning for canonical memory:
- ICP traits;
- winning/losing hooks and offers;
- objections;
- proof types;
- KPI outcomes;
- production time/cost;
- reusable prompts/workflows;
- reasons to stop/park.

Do not leak one client's private data into another client's context.

## Suggested temporary staffing

Use only roles needed for the current slice:

- Scout
- Commercial Analyst
- Creative/Offer Strategist
- Maker
- Taste Judge
- Evidence Judge
- Outreach Writer
- Measurement Analyst
- Memory Curator

These are temporary worker shapes, not new permanent agents. The builder and final judge remain separate.

## Automation boundary

### Auto
Hermes may automatically perform L0/L1 work such as public research, qualification, briefs, internal proof drafts, outreach drafts, tracking plans, metric analysis, proposal drafts, and learning proposals.

### Standing-policy only
Bounded reversible external L2 actions require a previously approved policy defining channel, recipient class, volume, budget, claims, and stop conditions.

### Human gate by default
Require approval for external sends/publishing, ad launches/spend, contracts/financial commitments, production deploys/migrations, destructive/admin/secrets/legal actions, or any action outside the current autonomy envelope.

## Default daily SELL slice

1. Find 10 evidence-backed candidates.
2. Qualify and rank them.
3. Pick the top 3 by value x fit x time-to-proof.
4. Build one small proof brief per target.
5. Produce up to 3 proof assets using capabilities already available.
6. Run independent taste + evidence review.
7. Prepare approval packets for external action.
8. After authorized tests, ingest outcomes.
9. End with PASS / ITERATE / PARK and exactly one recommended next action.

If the bottleneck is sales, outreach, approval, or testing, do not create more software or more proof assets merely to stay busy.

## Completion output

Return:

`OUTCOME | TARGETS | PROOF ASSETS | JUDGE SCORES | EVIDENCE | KPI/BASELINE | COST | WHAT CHANGED | RISKS | ROLLBACK | NEXT | HUMAN DECISION`

When evidence is weak, PARK it. Never turn uncertainty into additional architecture.
