The Power of Modular Writing

By writing modules as independent HTML fragments, the AI can focus on one concept at a time. This prevents "context drift" and ensures every module is high-quality.

💡
The "Lego" Strategy

Just like building with Legos, if one piece is wrong, you can just swap out that module's HTML file and rebuild. You don't have to rewrite the entire course.
