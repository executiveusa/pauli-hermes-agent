# Riverside Flow ICM Context

## Mode
Brownfield extension of Hermes. Do not alter Hermes core agent loop, gateway, model routing, or upstream-sensitive plumbing to implement Riverside workflows.

## Outcome
Create a repeatable system that converts Riverside conversations into measurable fundraising, sales, recruiting, partnership, documentary, and content outcomes.

## Target
Hermes/Cosmos operators working with New World Kids, social-impact ventures, studio clients, founders, community leaders, donors, mentors, creators, and documentary subjects.

## Constraints
- Riverside may be used manually before any API integration exists.
- API credentials are future wiring and must remain environment-only.
- Existing specialized skills remain authoritative for fundraising, social publishing, cinematic editing, and gauntlet review.
- No public release without the applicable approval policy.
- No claim is promoted from inference to fact without evidence.

## Proof
A successful run has a source receipt, narrative spine, derivative manifest, A/B hypothesis, engagement receipts, relationship/opportunity follow-up, and a 7-day learning decision.

## Commercial value
Priority order:
1. qualified donor/sponsor/partner opportunity;
2. paid Founder/Impact Story Sprint or recurring content client;
3. qualified service lead;
4. mentor/recruiting/community match;
5. reusable high-performing content intelligence.

## ICM stages

### 00_intake
Record purpose, guest, audience, primary CTA, target outcome, consent boundary, channels, and proof targets.

### 01_guest_strategy
Research why the guest matters, mutual value, likely topics, relationship objective, and useful follow-up. Never manipulate or conceal material intent.

### 02_record
Prepare Riverside studio/session, tech checklist, roles, recording consent, backup plan, and source identifiers. Keep raw/source media immutable.

### 03_extract
Capture transcript, timestamps, themes, quotes, claims, hooks, objections, opportunities, introductions, resources, and unresolved facts. Label each item VERIFIED, GUEST-STATED, INFERRED, or UNKNOWN.

### 04_narrative
Build and lock the Narrative Spine. Identify the strongest truthful story angle, the audience takeaway, proof, tension, payoff, and CTA. Reject misleading edits.

### 05_repurpose
Create long-form and short-form derivatives. Maintain source lineage for quotes and clips. Route premium edits to cinematic-master-editor and interactive pieces to social-drop-factory.

### 06_distribute
Package for each approved destination. Route social execution to scroll-media-operator. Publishing stays human-gated unless a governed destination policy exists.

### 07_ab_test
Run one-variable experiments where possible: hook, title, thumbnail, opening 3 seconds, duration, caption treatment, CTA wording, or release window. Do not change factual meaning between A and B.

### 08_learn
Collect 24h, 72h, 7d, and when useful 30d results. Separate signal from noise. Record winning pattern and failed hypothesis.

### 09_relationship_followup
Convert attention into action: thank-you, introduction, donor/client/partner next step, mentor match, collaboration, meeting, proposal, or nurture action. Relationship outcomes outrank vanity metrics.

## Evidence labels
- VERIFIED: direct platform/source evidence.
- GUEST-STATED: statement attributable to guest, not independently verified.
- INFERRED: reasonable interpretation, never presented as fact.
- UNKNOWN: insufficient evidence.

## Stop conditions
Stop and escalate before publishing if:
- consent is ambiguous;
- a minor/youth identity or sensitive detail may be exposed;
- a clip changes the speaker's meaning;
- a factual claim lacks support;
- an A/B variant relies on deception;
- a CTA implies a partnership, endorsement, donation, or result that does not exist;
- source media or transcript provenance is missing for a material quote.
