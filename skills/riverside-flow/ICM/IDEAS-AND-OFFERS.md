# Riverside Flow — 10 Locked Use Cases

Use these as reusable plays. Every play must define audience, outcome, CTA, consent boundary, source receipts, and relationship follow-up before distribution.

## 1. New World Kids Opportunity Show
Interview people creating real pathways for young people: coaches, artists, employers, educators, founders, mentors, athletes, philanthropists, and technologists.

Primary outcomes: donor relationships, mentor recruitment, public credibility, partner development.
Primary CTA: one action per episode such as donate, mentor, introduce, sponsor, or apply.

## 2. 12 Kids / 12 Stories
Document the first cohort through consent-safe, dignity-preserving stories focused on interests, paid opportunity, mentorship, completed work, and next steps.

Primary outcomes: fundraising proof, sponsor retention, recruitment, annual-report assets.
Constraint: minors and vulnerable youth require stricter consent and identity/privacy handling.

## 3. Coaches Who Changed Lives
Interview coaches and sports mentors about development beyond the game.

Primary outcomes: sports-network expansion, mentor recruitment, sponsor conversations, audience growth.

## 4. Founder Story Sprint
Paid 45-90 minute founder interview converted into a long-form story, short clips, article/newsletter, social posts, website messaging source, and CTA package.

Primary outcomes: immediate studio revenue and qualified Vibe Audit / Rescue / Launch leads.
Commercial hypothesis: sell as a bounded content product before building custom software.

## 5. Funder Conversations
Interview philanthropists, foundations, fiscal sponsors, impact investors, and community funders about what they want to see funded and why.

Primary outcomes: relationship intelligence, trusted access, introductions, future funding conversations.
Constraint: never disguise a material fundraising ask or misrepresent editorial independence.

## 6. Seattle Opportunity Roundtable
Bring 2-4 local leaders together around one concrete youth/community opportunity problem.

Primary outcomes: sponsors, referrals, partnerships, local authority, event-derived content.

## 7. Nonprofit Founder Spotlight
Interview nonprofit leaders about mission, operating constraints, fundraising, technology, and what they need next.

Primary outcomes: content plus qualified leads for Vibe Audit, Rescue, Sovereign Launch, or MAXX Operations.

## 8. Youth Career AMA
Bring working professionals and mentors together to answer practical questions about turning an existing interest into paid work, education, apprenticeship, or portfolio proof.

Primary outcomes: youth value, mentor recruitment, sponsor relevance, reusable educational library.

## 9. AI for Good / Sovereign Tech Interviews
Interview builders and operators about practical AI, ownership, automation, local/sovereign systems, nonprofit applications, and responsible deployment.

Primary outcomes: studio authority, partnerships, technical leads, contributor recruitment.

## 10. Bilingual US-Mexico Creator / Impact Series
Capture English and Spanish conversations with creators, founders, nonprofits, community leaders, and social-purpose ventures across both markets.

Primary outcomes: cross-border partnerships, bilingual client acquisition, expanded distribution, cultural/network leverage.

# Five highest-value operating modes

1. Relationship-first fundraising engine.
2. Founder/Impact Story Sprint paid offer.
3. New World Kids flagship owned-media network.
4. Documentary research + interview ingestion layer.
5. Live roundtables/webinars that become content and leads.

# Black Swan rule

Do not optimize only for downloads/views. Treat the guest network and structured transcript intelligence as the compounding asset.

For every interview extract:
- expertise;
- organization;
- resources;
- needs;
- interests;
- funding interests;
- mentor capability;
- youth/community opportunities;
- potential introductions;
- geographic reach;
- relationship next action.

Use this to build an Opportunity Graph over time. The graph is not permission to expose private information or infer sensitive traits.
