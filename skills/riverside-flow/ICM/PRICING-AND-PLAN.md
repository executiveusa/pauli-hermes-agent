# Riverside Pricing + Plan Decision

Last verified: 2026-08-23
Primary source: https://riverside.com/pricing
Nonprofit support source: https://support.riverside.fm/hc/en-us/articles/11084604328221-Do-you-have-coupons-discounts-or-promotions

## Current public pricing

- Free: $0/month.
- Pro: $29 month-to-month or $24/month billed annually ($288/year).
- Grow: $39 month-to-month or $34/month billed annually ($408/year).
- Webinar: $99 month-to-month or $79/month billed annually (annual-equivalent $948/year).
- Business: custom pricing.

Pricing changes. Hermes must re-verify the official pricing page before purchase, renewal, or quoting a client.

## Current recommendation

Default: **Grow**, but do not buy annually until the workflow proves value or a nonprofit discount has been checked.

Why Grow:
- everything in Pro;
- 2 studios;
- social scheduling;
- newsletter;
- creator website;
- podcast hosting for 2 shows;
- AI thumbnails;
- 1080p livestreaming;
- multistreaming;
- 20 hours/month of separate-track downloads.

Use Pro when the need is primarily high-quality recording/editing/repurposing and one studio is enough.

Use Webinar only when structured lead-generation webinars are a real active channel and registration, Q&A/polls, reminder/follow-up emails, webinar analytics, or CRM lead capture justify the price.

Use Business only after a verified requirement for API access, enterprise collaboration, advanced controls, high-scale webinars, or other Business-only features. Do not upgrade merely to avoid a manual step before ROI is proven.

## Nonprofit path

Riverside support explicitly tells students and nonprofit organizations to contact Support about discounts. No fixed public nonprofit percentage is guaranteed.

Before annual purchase for New World Kids or another eligible nonprofit:
1. Contact Riverside Support.
2. Explain nonprofit status and intended youth/social-impact use.
3. Ask for available nonprofit pricing and eligibility requirements.
4. Save the offer/receipt in the run record.
5. Compare discounted annual cost with a month-to-month pilot.

## AI-credit caution

Riverside states that some AI features such as AI Translation, AI B-Roll, and Animated Clips use AI credits, while core features such as Magic Clips, Magic Audio, and Co-Creator do not. Hermes should track AI-credit use separately from subscription cost when evaluating unit economics.

## Commercial unit-economics rule

For every paid content offer, track:

`gross revenue - Riverside attributable cost - editing/AI cost - distribution cost - human labor cost = contribution margin`

Do not claim high margin until the real delivery cost is measured.
