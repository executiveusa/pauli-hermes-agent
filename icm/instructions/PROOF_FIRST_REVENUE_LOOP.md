# Proof-First Revenue Loop — Canonical Commercial Contract

## Inputs
- one active `SELL` workstream;
- public/authorized prospect evidence;
- existing skills/tools/capabilities;
- current opportunity/test record when continuing a run.

## Process
Execute the numbered ICM pipeline at `../workflows/proof-first-revenue-loop/`:
`01_scan -> 02_qualify -> 03_model -> 04_prove -> 05_judge -> 06_approve -> 07_test -> 08_verify -> 09_close -> 10_scale -> 11_compound`.

State transitions require receipts. Missing proof means remain, iterate, or park.

## Laws
- proof before pitch;
- cash before more code;
- inspect and reuse before adding;
- analyze competitor patterns, never copy protected creative or misrepresent provenance;
- never fabricate performance, customers, testimonials, or guarantees;
- define KPI/baseline/threshold before winner claims;
- builder cannot approve itself;
- external sending/publishing/spend/commitments are human-gated unless a standing policy exists;
- scale only verified winners;
- preserve owner/client control of accounts, code, data, credentials, domains, hosting, and final commitments;
- do not create a fourth active workstream.

## Default bounded slice
Start with 10 evidence-backed prospects and at most 3 proof assets. Increase volume only after response/test quality is verified.

## Outputs
`OUTCOME | TARGETS | PROOF ASSETS | JUDGE SCORES | EVIDENCE | KPI/BASELINE | COST | WHAT CHANGED | RISKS | ROLLBACK | NEXT | HUMAN DECISION`

## Failure / stop
Park when evidence is weak, the bottleneck is human approval/outreach/testing, the cost/risk ceiling is exceeded, or no measurable proof path exists. Do not turn uncertainty into more architecture.

## Human check
Approve the exact target, proof asset, claim/message, channel, spend ceiling, KPI plan, and rollback before any gated external action.
