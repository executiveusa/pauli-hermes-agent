# 06_approve — prepare the action gate

## Inputs
- Working: `../05_judge/output/judgment.md` and accepted proof artifacts
- Reference: `../../../instructions/PROOF_FIRST_REVENUE_LOOP.md`
- Reference: `../../../context/ENVELOPES.md`

## Process
1. Package target, proof, exact claims/message, channel, recipient/audience, cost ceiling, KPI/test plan, risk tier, rollback and stop condition.
2. Determine whether a standing policy authorizes the action.
3. Treat drafting as internal work, never as implicit permission to send.

## Outputs
- `output/approval-packet.md`

## Human check
Approve any L3/L4 action and any L2 action lacking explicit standing policy.
