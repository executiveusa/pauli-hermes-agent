# 02_qualify — rank opportunities

## Inputs
- Working: 01_scan candidate list
- Reference: `../../../context/TASK_PROFILES.md`

## Process
1. Score problem value, ability to pay, reachability, fit, time-to-proof, KPI clarity, and risk.
2. Select the top 3 for proof production.
3. Record why the rest are deferred or parked.

## Outputs
Ranked shortlist -> active opportunity/mission product state.

## Human check
Verify the top 3 are commercially plausible and no fourth workstream is being created.
