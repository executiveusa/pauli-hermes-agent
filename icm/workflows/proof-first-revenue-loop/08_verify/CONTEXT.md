# 08_verify — decide from real outcomes

## Inputs
- Working: `../07_test/output/test-record.md` plus actual authorized metrics
- Reference: `../../../instructions/PROOF_FIRST_REVENUE_LOOP.md`

## Process
1. Compare the actual KPI to the predeclared baseline and threshold.
2. Verify attribution and important failure paths.
3. Return PASS, ITERATE or PARK. No real measurement means no winner claim.

## Outputs
- `output/verification.md`

## Human check
Escalate only when evidence is too ambiguous for a consequential claim.
