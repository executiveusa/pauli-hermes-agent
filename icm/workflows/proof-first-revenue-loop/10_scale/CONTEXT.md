# 10_scale — expand only a verified winner

## Inputs

- Working: `../08_verify/output/verification.md`

## Process
1. Increase variants, qualified outreach, worker capacity, channel coverage, automation or price only when evidence supports it.
2. Set ceiling, rollback and stop condition for every increase.
3. Do not scale around unresolved attribution or negative unit economics.

## Outputs
- `output/scale-plan.md`

## Human check
Approve any expansion of spend, external authority or commitments beyond standing policy.
