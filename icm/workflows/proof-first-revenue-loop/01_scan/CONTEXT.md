# 01_scan — find evidence-backed prospects

## Inputs
- Reference: `../../../instructions/PROOF_FIRST_REVENUE_LOOP.md`
- Working: current SELL workstream/market scope

## Process
1. Find public/authorized prospects with an observable business gap and ability to act.
2. Capture evidence refs and a constraint hypothesis.
3. Stop at 10 qualified-enough candidates for the bounded slice.

## Outputs
Candidate list -> active opportunity/mission product state.

## Human check
No human gate for public research; verify no private/unauthorized data was used before advancing.
