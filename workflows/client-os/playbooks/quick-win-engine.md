# Quick Win Engine

## Purpose
Select the first intervention that earns trust with measurable proof.

## Candidate test
For each recommendation score 1-5:
- Impact
- Cost (reverse scored: lower cost = higher score)
- Speed
- Proof quality
- Reversibility

Reject work that requires a rebuild before the business problem is proven.

## Selection rule
Choose one primary quick win and at most two supporting actions. The primary quick win must have a named metric, baseline, owner, time box, and rollback.

## Commercial gate
Prefer actions that can demonstrate revenue, qualified demand, conversion, retention, or material savings.

## Record
Create an `experiment-plan.json` before execution and update it with the result after measurement.
