# Monthly Growth Loop

1. Reconcile posted evidence and current metrics.
2. Close completed experiments with result and confidence.
3. Record what changed in client memory.
4. Identify the largest remaining revenue or trust leak.
5. Select the next reversible experiment.
6. Produce the monthly growth report.
7. Escalate decisions requiring client approval.

## Guardrails
- Do not confuse activity with impact.
- Do not keep failed experiments alive without new evidence.
- Preserve a clear rollback path.
- Keep no more than three active client growth experiments unless explicitly approved.

## Output
Update `monthly-growth-report.json`, relevant experiment records, and client decisions.
