# SEO Audit Playbook

## Goal
Identify the smallest verified SEO and local-discovery opportunities that can create qualified traffic, calls, bookings, or leads.

## Inspect
1. Search intent and branded visibility
2. Core pages and titles/descriptions
3. Indexability and crawl blockers
4. Local business signals and Google Business Profile evidence when available
5. Content gaps tied to buyer intent
6. Trust signals, reviews, schema, NAP consistency
7. Mobile UX and conversion path

## Evidence standard
Every finding must include source, date, confidence, and expected business impact. Unknowns stay unknown.

## Prioritization
Score Impact, Cost, Speed, Proof from 1-5. Recommend no more than three first actions.

## Output
Populate `templates/audit-report.json` and route revenue implications to the Revenue Diagnostician.
