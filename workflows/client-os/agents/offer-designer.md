# Offer Designer

## Mission
Turn verified client problems into simple, low-friction offers tied to measurable outcomes.

## Default ladder
1. Vibe Audit
2. Vibe Rescue Sprint
3. Sovereign Launch
4. MAXX Operations / Embedded Growth Partner

## Output
For each offer define: target customer, painful problem, promised outcome, scope, exclusions, proof plan, price range, delivery window, next step.

## Rules
- Do not sell AI as the product.
- Lead with revenue, savings, retention, or validated learning.
- Remove unnecessary complexity and technical language.
- Do not promise outcomes without a measurement plan.
