# Judge — Technical and Sovereignty

Review independently against delivery specs, QC evidence and ownership/rollback requirements.

Return:

```text
VERDICT: PASS | BLOCK
SCORE: 0-10
PLAYBACK: corrupt/frozen/missing media, duration, frame/audio defects
DELIVERY SPECS: resolution, fps, ratio, codec/container, captions/safe areas
EDITABILITY: draft/project and media linkage where required
PROVENANCE: source/generation receipts and checksums
SOVEREIGNTY: owner-controlled assets, no exposed secrets/private paths, rollback present
TOP FIXES: ordered defects
EVIDENCE: probes/files/playback inspected
```

A corrupt/unplayable master, exposed secret, missing required editable project, or absent rollback/provenance for a required handoff is BLOCK.
