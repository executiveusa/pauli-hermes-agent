# Cinematic Production Quality Bar

A release candidate must score at least 8.5/10 overall and receive PASS from every independent judge.

## Story / Audience
- opening establishes a reason to continue appropriate to format;
- sequence has clear causality or information progression;
- ending resolves/payoffs the promised experience or CTA;
- retained shots are not redundant.

## Direction / Taste
- shots are physically specific and intentionally framed;
- camera motion is motivated by a change in story state or attention;
- visual references serve a production problem rather than generic style imitation;
- the cut does not rely on random AI b-roll to simulate production value.

## Continuity / Integrity
- identity, wardrobe, props, geography, light, screen direction and voice remain coherent where continuity applies;
- factual claims, quotes, labels and demonstrations remain supported and not misleading;
- brand and rights constraints are respected.

## Sound / Rhythm
- spoken audio is intelligible;
- music does not mask weak structure or dialogue;
- ambience/SFX/foley support pictured actions;
- rough-cut structure and fine-cut polish are judged separately;
- pacing matches the surface and audience rather than a universal fast-cut rule.

## Technical / Sovereignty
- master plays correctly and matches target format;
- captions are accurate, timed and safe;
- no unresolved placeholders, accidental watermarks or missing media;
- editable draft/project, source manifest, prompts/logs and rollback notes are retained when required;
- no secrets/private local paths are exposed in deliverables.

Critical factual, rights, secret exposure, missing-media, corrupt-render, or unplayable-output defects are automatic BLOCK regardless of score.
