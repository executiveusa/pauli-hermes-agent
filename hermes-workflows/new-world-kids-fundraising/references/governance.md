# Governance — New World Kids fundraising

## Fiscal sponsor
Humanitarian Social Innovations (HSI) is the fiscal sponsor for the sponsored New World Kids program. Treat HSI's current program-resource/onboarding instructions and the signed fiscal sponsorship agreement as governing references for fundraising/grant operations.

## Non-negotiable gates
- All grants require HSI staff review before program submission.
- Public legal/tax wording must describe the fiscal-sponsor relationship accurately; do not present HSI's EIN or federal recognition as New World Kids' own.
- Fundraising source outreach/materials must follow HSI approval requirements.
- Owner approval is required for payment/payout settings, campaign launch/unlaunch, material public claims, high-volume outbound communications, and financial commitments.

## GrantStation
GrantStation's published Terms of Use prohibit robots/automatic devices and AI/web-scraping/data-mining of the member service without prior written permission. Hermes must not automate GrantStation browser research. A human may use the authorized interface and pass opportunity details to Hermes for qualification/drafting.

## FundRazr
HSI provides access to FundRazr. Prefer FundRazr native features, authorized integrations/Zapier, and documented API access. Browser control may assist human-supervised setup but must not expose credentials or silently change payout, legal, tax-receipt, or launch settings.

## Secrets
Credentials belong in local secret storage or environment variables, never Git, memory markdown, screenshots, logs, issue bodies, or prompts persisted to disk.
