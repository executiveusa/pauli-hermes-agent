# 05_distribution — drive qualified traffic

## Inputs
- approved FundRazr URL and campaign copy
- approved media and audience permissions
- current channel history
- `../references/governance.md`

## Process
1. Build email, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, partner, and website variants.
2. Use a canonical campaign URL with channel-specific UTM tags.
3. Plan launch, evidence/update, reminder, and follow-up waves without manufactured urgency.
4. Route sensitive donor/funder replies to a human.
5. Send/publish at scale only after owner approval and through authorized tools.

## Outputs
- `output/channel-plan.md`
- `output/content-queue.md`
- `output/utm-map.md`

## Human check
Owner approves audiences, sequence, claims, and paid spend.
