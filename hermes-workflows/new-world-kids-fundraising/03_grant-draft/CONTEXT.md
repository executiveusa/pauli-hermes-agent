# 03_grant-draft — draft from evidence

## Inputs
- `../02_grant-research/output/shortlist.md`
- funder questions/guidelines
- program evidence, budget, required attachments
- `../references/governance.md`

## Process
1. Preserve the funder's actual questions and constraints.
2. Build a claim ledger before prose; mark unsupported items `NEEDS SOURCE`.
3. Draft to funder priorities without changing the mission to chase funding.
4. Build the budget from real planned costs and sponsor requirements.
5. Check word counts, attachments, deadline, eligibility, and duplicate submissions.

## Outputs
- `output/claim-ledger.md`
- `output/grant-draft.md`
- `output/attachments-checklist.md`

## Human check
Owner verifies commitments, facts, people, budget, and requested amount before HSI review.
