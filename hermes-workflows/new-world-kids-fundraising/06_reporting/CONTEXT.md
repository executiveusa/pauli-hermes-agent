# 06_reporting — reconcile results and learn

## Inputs
- authorized FundRazr reports/integration output
- UTM/channel analytics
- grant pipeline + HSI review status
- campaign costs/paid spend

## Process
1. Reconcile gross contributions, fees, net, donor count, recurring gifts, refunds/failed payments, and date range.
2. Attribute traffic/conversions only where analytics support it.
3. Keep grant statuses distinct: researched, drafted, HSI-approved, submitted, awarded, declined, paid.
4. Record what worked, failed, and remains unknown.
5. Feed only verified lessons into the next run.

## Outputs
- `output/fundraising-report.md`
- `output/grant-pipeline-report.md`
- `output/lessons.md`

## Human check
Owner verifies totals against source records before they become public claims or planning assumptions.
