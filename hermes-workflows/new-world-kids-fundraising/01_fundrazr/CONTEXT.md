# 01_fundrazr — build and maintain the campaign

## Inputs
- `../references/governance.md`
- current New World Kids mission/program evidence
- campaign need, budget, approved media, and HSI/account constraints

## Process
1. Define the campaign outcome, goal, audience, and evidence.
2. Draft title, story, media plan, updates, SEO, sharing copy, and UTM map.
3. Prefer native/authorized FundRazr integration paths; browser control is supervised setup/verification only.
4. Keep payout, tax/legal, and launch-state changes behind owner confirmation.
5. Capture the canonical FundRazr URL and current campaign state.

## Outputs
- `output/campaign-brief.md`
- `output/campaign-copy.md`
- `output/launch-checklist.md`

## Human check
Owner confirms campaign facts, media rights, destination, settings, and launch.
