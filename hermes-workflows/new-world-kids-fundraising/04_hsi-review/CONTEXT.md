# 04_hsi-review — obtain sponsor approval

## Inputs
- `../03_grant-draft/output/grant-draft.md`
- `../03_grant-draft/output/claim-ledger.md`
- `../03_grant-draft/output/attachments-checklist.md`
- `../references/governance.md`

## Process
1. Assemble the exact application, budget, funder URL, deadline, requested amount, and attachments.
2. Highlight fiscal-sponsor/legal/tax/banking/reporting representations.
3. Send/prepare the packet through HSI's current review channel before funder submission.
4. Record feedback and revise as required.
5. Mark approved only when actual HSI approval exists.

## Outputs
- `output/review-packet.md`
- `output/review-status.md`

## Human check
Owner verifies recorded HSI approval. Hermes may not submit while review is pending or changes-requested.
