# 02_grant-research — qualify opportunities

## Inputs
- `../references/governance.md`
- current program priorities, geography, applicant/fiscal-sponsor structure, budget range, deadlines

## Process
1. Research public/authorized sources and keep source URL + retrieval date.
2. Do not automate GrantStation; normalize opportunity details supplied by a human using the authorized interface.
3. Check eligibility before scoring.
4. Score mission fit, evidence readiness, burden, timing, amount, and relationship value.
5. Reject weak fits explicitly.

## Outputs
- `output/opportunities.md`
- `output/shortlist.md`

## Human check
Owner chooses which opportunities move to drafting.
